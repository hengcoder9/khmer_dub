Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · គោលការណ៍ឯកជនភាព

KhmerDub Studio ("we", "the app") is a Windows desktop app for dubbing videos into Khmer, with an online account at miennams.net for sign-in, free trials and paid plans. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account details. When you create an account we store your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you sign in with Telegram, we receive your Telegram user ID, name, username and profile picture from Telegram. We never receive your Google or Telegram password.
  • Password. If you set a password, we store only a secure one-way hash of it.
  • Plan and payments. Your plan, trial dates, orders (amount, currency, status, order reference) and license codes you redeem. Payments are made in your banking app by KHQR; we do not receive or store card or bank account numbers.
  • Sign-in sessions. The device name, IP address and times of your sign-ins, so we can keep you signed in, limit the number of devices per account and protect against misuse.
  • API keys you choose to save. If you save your own AI service keys (for example Google Gemini) to your account, they are stored encrypted so you can use them on your other PCs.

2. What stays on your computer

Your videos, audio, subtitles, translations and projects are processed and stored on your own PC. They are not uploaded to our server. When you use an AI feature, the text or media needed for that task is sent directly from your PC to the AI service you selected (such as Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude or OpenRouter) under that service's own terms and privacy policy.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To give you your free trial and the plan you paid for, and to confirm payments.
  • To show your name and picture in the app.
  • To provide support and prevent fraud or abuse.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as described below.

4. Who we share it with

  • Google and Telegram, only to verify your sign-in when you choose those buttons.
  • Payment providers (such as AnajakPay / ABA KHQR), only the order amount and reference needed to process and confirm your payment.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the account database on our behalf.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

5. Google user data

KhmerDub Studio requests only the basic Google scopes openid, email and profile. We use this data only to sign you in and show your name and picture in the app. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account exists. Sign-in sessions expire automatically, and expired sign-in links are deleted. Payment records may be kept as long as needed for accounting. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal information, except records we must keep by law.

7. Security

The server is reached only over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, saved API keys and sign-in secrets are encrypted, and on your PC your sign-in is protected by Windows encryption. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Your choices

  • You can see and change your name and password in the app (Account).
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your account and data at any time.
  • You can remove KhmerDub Studio's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Children

KhmerDub Studio is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Important changes will be announced in the app.

11. Contact

For questions or to delete your account, contact us through the support contact shown in the app (Account → Plans), or visit miennams.net.
